Pods are disposable, so you rarely create one directly. You create a
<Term>Deployment</Term>, which owns a <Term>ReplicaSet</Term>, which keeps a
stated number of pods alive.

The mechanism underneath is <Term>reconciliation</Term>: a
<Term>controller</Term> compares what you asked for against what exists and
closes the gap, over and over. Nothing is assumed to have worked.

Changing the pod template starts a <Term>rollout</Term>, shifting traffic to new
pods gradually instead of restarting everything at once.
