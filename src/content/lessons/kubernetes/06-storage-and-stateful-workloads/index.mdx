Everything so far assumed workloads were interchangeable. Databases are not.

A <Term>PersistentVolume</Term> is storage whose lifetime is independent of any
pod. Workloads do not reference it directly; they make a
<Term>PersistentVolumeClaim</Term> describing what they need, and a
<Term>StorageClass</Term> explains how to provision it on demand.

For workloads where each replica is distinct, a <Term>StatefulSet</Term> gives
every pod a stable identity and its own claim.
